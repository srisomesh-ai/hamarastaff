How HamaraStaff works

Your team's entire field day — captured automatically from their phone. No registers, no phone calls, no guesswork.

📲
1
Staff starts the day

Employee taps "Start My Day" on their phone. Time and GPS location are recorded — that's their attendance, marked Present automatically.

📍
2
Visits are tracked live

Each client visit is a task. On arrival they tap "I've Reached" — the exact location and time are stamped on the visit trail.

📝
3
Visit closed with report

After the meeting, staff fill a short form — person met, products discussed, client remarks — and the report is emailed to the client instantly.

💼
4
HOD approves payroll

Management reviews the monthly GPS attendance on the desktop panel, approves it in one click, and downloads the Excel for payroll processing.
